Projects

Selected computational materials research.

Current projects span dental polymer discovery, crosslinked-network simulation, and experiment-grounded modeling of amorphous silica.

Project 01 · In progress

AI-guided discovery of BPA-free dental resin backbones

This project uses multi-objective active learning to prioritize BPA-free backbones from a chemically filtered molecular library. Candidate evaluation combines virtual curing with cured-network property calculations.

ObjectivesHigh modulus · Low shrinkage · Low FFV
OracleHTPolyNet and GROMACS virtual curing
LearningGPR surrogates and random scalarization

Research goal: identify chemically realistic tradeoffs rather than a single mathematically optimal molecule.

Project 02 · Published

Long-range ML potentials and medium-range order in silica glass

This work compares short-range and long-range machine-learning potentials against neutron and high-energy X-ray diffraction. The analysis extends beyond local bond lengths and angles to network topology and medium-range order.

SimulationLarge-scale melt, quench, and glass MD
StructureRDFs · S(Q) · angles · rings · topology
ValidationNeutron and X-ray diffraction

Project 03 · Molecular simulation

Structure–property modeling of crosslinked polymer networks

This project develops reproducible virtual-curing and post-simulation workflows for extracting cured-network properties across independent replicas. It provides the simulation foundation for the dental-resin discovery workflow.

NetworkReaction-driven thermoset construction
PropertiesElastic response · Tg · FFV · shrinkage
WorkflowAutomated replicas and HPC execution

Research goal: connect molecular architecture and cure evolution to reproducible, experimentally relevant polymer properties.
